1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the MVPTimer Discord bot (“Bot”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when it is used in Discord servers.

By using the Bot, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following types of information:

  • Discord user IDs, server IDs, role IDs, channel IDs, and command usage data needed for Bot functionality.
  • Basic account information provided by Discord when the Bot is invoked in a server, such as username and user ID.
  • Message content or command arguments when they are required to perform a command requested by a user.
  • Usage metadata such as timestamps, command names, server and channel identifiers, and success or error logs for troubleshooting and reliability.
  • Preferences or configuration settings saved for a server, such as timers, reminders, or Bot configuration.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide the Bot’s timer and reminder functionality.
  • Process commands and respond to users in Discord.
  • Maintain server-specific settings and Bot behavior.
  • Diagnose errors, fix bugs, and improve reliability.
  • Prevent abuse, spam, or misuse of the Bot.

4. Data Storage and Retention

We store only the data needed to provide the Bot’s services. Information is retained only as long as necessary for operational, troubleshooting, or legal purposes.

If a server or user requests deletion of Bot-related data, we will make reasonable efforts to remove that data when technically feasible, subject to any legal or operational requirements that require retention.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share data only with:

  • Discord or hosting providers necessary to operate the Bot.
  • Third-party service providers used for hosting, logging, or analytics where required for operation.
  • Authorities or legal entities when required by law or to protect against abuse, fraud, or safety risks.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

7. User Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights related to access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your data. If you want to request information about your data or ask for deletion, contact the Bot Owner using the information below.
